OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R09 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Peach-leaf extract: UV models and AMPK–Nrf2 signaling

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R09 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Eun Ok Kim, Dae Geon Lee, Cheol Jong Jung, Yeong Eun Yu, Museok Hong, Il Je Cho, Sae Kwang Ku. “Prunus persica Leaf Extract Mitigates Ultraviolet-Induced Skin Damage Via Activation of AMP-Activated Protein Kinase.” Natural Product Communications 20(4) (2025), article 1934578X251330952. DOI: 10.1177/1934578X251330952.

Publisher DOI · Publisher record

Material and model

The material AstraPL was a Prunus persica (peach) leaf extract.

The study examined UV-exposed HaCaT keratinocytes and UV-exposed SKH1 hairless mice. In the mouse experiment the extract was applied topically, with an ascorbic-acid comparator.

Reported observations

The publisher abstract describes cell-viability and AMPKα/Nrf2 signaling changes and lower UV-associated dryness, epidermal thickness and wrinkle measures in mice. AMPK inhibition blocked the reported AstraPL-associated Nrf2 nuclear accumulation and antioxidant-gene induction in the cell experiment.
