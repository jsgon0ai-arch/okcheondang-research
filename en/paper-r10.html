OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R10 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Aeration of red-ginseng powder extract

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R10 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Ryu Hee-Jeong, Jung Chul-Jong, Seo Jeong-Gyun, Li Xian, Yu Yeong-Eun, Beik Gyung-Yun. “Effect of aeration process on changes of prosapogenin content and antioxidant activity of red ginseng powder extract.” Korean Journal of Food Science and Technology 51(6), 576–583 (2019). DOI: 10.9721/KJFST.2019.51.6.576.

Publisher DOI · KoreaScience article

Material and model

Red-ginseng powder extract was processed with and without aeration. KoreaScience lists all six authors with the Okchundang R&D laboratory affiliation.

The analytical study compared extraction time, color values, total polyphenols and flavonoids, ginsenosides Rb1/Rg1/Rg3, and DPPH, ABTS and H₂O₂-scavenging measurements.

Reported observations

The abstract reports higher antioxidant-assay readings and a higher Rg3 amount after aeration than without aeration.
