OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R12 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Crataegus fruit and scopolamine-induced memory measures

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R12 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jihye Lee, Sang Hyeok Park, Chanhee Lee, Hye-Jeong Kim, Chul Jong Jung, Gyung Yun Beik, Jin Gi Shin, Ji Wook Jung. “인지 및 기억 개선 기능성 식품으로서 산사의 효과.” Food Science and Preservation 26(2), 238–245 (2019). DOI: 10.11002/kjfp.2019.26.2.238.

Publisher DOI · Publisher article

Material and model

This paper studied Crataegus pinnatifida extract. Its publisher lists Chul Jong Jung, Gyung Yun Beik and Jin Gi Shin with Okchundang and the other authors with Daegu Haany University.

Mice with scopolamine-induced memory impairment received the extract orally. Investigators used Y-maze, passive-avoidance and Morris-water-maze tests and measured brain acetylcholinesterase (AChE).

Reported observations

The abstract reports higher spontaneous alternation and passive-avoidance latency, lower escape latency, and lower brain AChE activity in the extract-treated groups than the challenged controls.
