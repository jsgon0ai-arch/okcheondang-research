OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R07 — RESEARCH RECORDS

A. stricta in OVA-challenged mice and stimulated cells

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R07 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Cheol-Jong Jung, Seok-Man Park, Dae-Geon Lee, Yeong-Eun Yu, Tae-Hun Ku, Im-Joung La, Il-Je Cho, Sae-Kwang Ku. “Adenophora Stricta Root Extract Alleviates Airway Inflammation in Mice with Ovalbumin-Induced Allergic Asthma.” Antioxidants 12(4) (2023), 922. DOI: 10.3390/antiox12040922.

Publisher DOI · Full text (PMC)

Material and model

The study used a spray-dried water extract (AsE) of Adenophora stricta root and examined it separately in mice and RAW264.7 cells.

Female C57BL/6 mice (ten per group) were sensitized and intranasally challenged with ovalbumin (OVA). AsE 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg was given orally daily on days 0–15, alongside an OVA control and a 0.75 mg/kg dexamethasone comparison group. The cell assay used LPS-stimulated RAW264.7 macrophages.

Reported observations

The paper reports reduced lung congestion and inflammatory-cell infiltration, serum OVA-specific IgE and lavage IL-4/IL-5 in mice. The cell experiment reports reduced NO, TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6 measures and changes in signaling-protein phosphorylation.
