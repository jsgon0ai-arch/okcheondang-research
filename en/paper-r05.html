OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R05 — RESEARCH RECORDS

A. stricta extract in a particulate-exposure mouse model

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R05 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Seok-Man Park, Cheol-Jong Jung, Dae-Geon Lee, Beom-Rak Choi, Tae-Hun Ku, Im-Joung La, Il-Je Cho, Sae-Kwang Ku. “Adenophora Stricta Root Extract Protects Lung Injury from Exposure to Particulate Matter 2.5 in Mice.” Antioxidants 11(7) (2022), 1376. DOI: 10.3390/antiox11071376.

Publisher DOI · Full text (PMC)

Material and model

Adenophora stricta root was extracted twice in hot water and spray-dried to produce AsE; the paper reports a 42.81% extraction yield. This is the A. stricta species.

Male BALB/c mice (ten per group) received 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg AsE orally for ten days. Diesel-derived particulate matter (1 mg/kg) was administered intranasally on days 0 and 2; a dexamethasone (0.75 mg/kg) group was included.

Reported observations

Compared with particulate-exposed controls, the paper reports lower relative lung weight and congestion, fewer inflammatory cells in bronchoalveolar lavage and changes in lung inflammation/oxidative-stress measures. Body weight did not differ with AsE treatment.
