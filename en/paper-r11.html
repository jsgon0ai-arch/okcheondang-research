OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R11 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Crataegus fruit in an amyloid-injection mouse model

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R11 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jihye Lee, Eunbi Cho, Huiyoung Kwon, Jieun Jeon, Chul Jong Jung, Minho Moon, Mira Jun, Young Choon Lee, Dong Hyun Kim, Ji Wook Jung. “The fruit of Crataegus pinnatifida ameliorates memory deficits in β-amyloid protein-induced Alzheimer's disease mouse model.” Journal of Ethnopharmacology 243, 112107 (2019). DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2019.112107.

Publisher DOI · Europe PMC abstract

Material and model

The study used a Crataegus pinnatifida fruit ethanol extract (CPE). The author-affiliation record identifies Chul Jong Jung with Okchundang.

Investigators examined memory-related behavioral measures and hippocampal tissue in mice injected intracerebroventricularly with amyloid-β; related assays studied amyloid aggregation and cell responses.

Reported observations

The abstract reports differences in memory-test performance, hippocampal glial activation, amyloid-fibril formation and cell-toxicity measures between extract-treated and model groups.
