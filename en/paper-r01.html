OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R01 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Adenophorae Radix powder and induced cough in mice

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R01 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jin-Ryul Hu, Chul-Jong Jung, Seong-Min Ku, Dae-Hwa Jung, Sae-Kwang Ku, Jae-Suk Choi. “Antitussive, expectorant, and anti-inflammatory effects of Adenophorae Radix powder in ICR mice.” Journal of Ethnopharmacology 239 (2019), 111915. DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2019.111915.

Publisher DOI · PubMed abstract

Material and model

The study material was Adenophorae Radix (sa-sam) powder.

ICR mice received 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg orally once daily for 11 days. Researchers recorded cough responses after ammonia exposure, tracheal lavage and airway tissue measures, and ear inflammation after xylene exposure; theobromine, ambroxol and dexamethasone served as test-specific comparators.

Reported observations

The PubMed abstract reports dose-related reductions in induced cough responses and ear-inflammation measures, together with changes in airway tissue and mucus-related measurements. The abstract reports a decrease in tracheal-lavage optical density rather than an increase.
