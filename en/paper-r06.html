OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R06 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Sasam-Kyeongokgo and gefitinib in tumor-bearing mice

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R06 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jeong-Hoon Oh, Joo Wan Kim, Chul-Jong Jung, Jae-Suk Choi, Sae Kwang Ku. “Oral Combination Treatment of Gefitinib (IressaTM) and Sasam-Kyeongokgo: Synergistic Effects on the NCI-H520 Tumor Cell Line.” Applied Sciences 13(2) (2023), 1090. DOI: 10.3390/app13021090.

Publisher DOI · Publisher record

Material and model

The study combined an experimental Sasam-Kyeongokgo (SKOG) preparation with gefitinib, and also compared gefitinib-alone and SKOG-alone groups.

Human NCI-H520 lung-cancer cells were implanted in immunodeficient nude mice. The study compared oral gefitinib alone, SKOG alone and combination groups, measuring tumor- and cachexia-related outcomes.

Reported observations

The publisher abstract reports tumor- and cachexia-related measurements and immune markers more favorable in the combination groups than with gefitinib alone; group-by-group effect sizes are not stated here.
