OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R04 — RESEARCH RECORDS

OCD20015-V009: influenza pre-treatment experiments

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R04 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Eun-Bin Kwon, You-Chang Oh, Youn-Hwan Hwang, Wei Li, Seok-Man Park, Ryong Kong, Young Soo Kim, Jang-Gi Choi. “A Herbal Mixture Formula of OCD20015-V009 Prophylactic Administration to Enhance Interferon-Mediated Antiviral Activity Against Influenza A Virus.” Frontiers in Pharmacology 12 (2021), 764297. DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2021.764297.

Publisher DOI · Publisher full text

Material and model

OCD20015-V009 was a water-extract mixture of seven botanical ingredients: ginseng, Poria, Rehmannia, Adenophora root, Platycodon root, hawthorn fruit and Astragalus root.

Mouse-derived RAW264.7 macrophages were pretreated before influenza A/PR/8/34 exposure. Female BALB/c mice (ten per group) received 100 or 300 mg/kg orally each day for seven days before infection; the study also compared simultaneous and post-infection treatment.

Reported observations

In pretreated cells, 50 and 100 μg/mL were associated with 81.5% and 91.1% lower viral-replication measures than infected controls. The paper also reports interferon-related signaling changes and a lower mortality measure in the 300 mg/kg pretreated mouse group; concurrent or post-infection treatment did not show antiviral activity in the reported experiments.
