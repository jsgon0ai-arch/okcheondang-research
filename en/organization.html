Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Explore Okcheondang’s research subjects, laboratory methods, publications and patent record.

Areas of inquiry

  • Traditional formula and botanical-material research: sa-sam, Gyeongokgo and separate extract preparations.
  • Airway and exposure models: experimental work on material-specific biological responses.
  • UV and skin models: peach-leaf and Elaeagnus fruit extracts studied as separate materials.

Research and quality methods

Okcheondang’s official quality-management page introduces these three categories of work at its affiliated R&D laboratory:

  • Constituent analysis: HPLC quantitation, moisture, UV absorbance and pH measurements.
  • Bioactivity analysis: ELISA, PCR and cell-based assays.
  • Formulation analysis: fluidized-bed process (FBS), disintegration/dissolution, controlled release and pharmacokinetic/PK studies.
