Registration KR102022325B1 describes a botanical composition and names its original applicants.

Patent publication

KR102022325B1, “Composition for antitussive, expectorant or anti-inflammatory use,” was filed 30 January 2018 and the B1 registration was published 18 September 2019. The original applicants shown are Okcheondang Co., Ltd. and agricultural corporation Wongwang Co., Ltd. The abstract and claims discuss ginseng, Adenophora triphylla, Poria, Rehmannia and honey.

Botanical material

The patent names Adenophora triphylla. Publications R05 and R07 instead examine Adenophora stricta.
