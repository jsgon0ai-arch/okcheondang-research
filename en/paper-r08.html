OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R08 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Elaeagnus fruit extract: oral dosing in photoexposed mice

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R08 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Seok-Man Park, Cheol-Jong Jung, Dae-Geon Lee, Yeong-Eun Yu, Tae-Hun Ku, Mu-Seok Hong, Tae-Kyung Lim, Kwong-Il Paeng, Hyun-Ki Cho, Il-Je Cho, Sae-Kwang Ku. “Elaeagnus umbellata Fruit Extract Protects Skin from Ultraviolet-Mediated Photoaging in Hairless Mice.” Antioxidants 13(2) (2024), 195. DOI: 10.3390/antiox13020195.

Publisher DOI · Full text (PMC)

Material and model

Elaeagnus umbellata fruit was extracted twice in boiling water, concentrated, combined with dextrin at 50% (w/w) and spray-dried as Elaea.

Female SKH1 hairless mice (ten per group) received ultraviolet exposure three times weekly for 15 weeks. Elaea 50, 100 or 200 mg/kg was given orally each day, with an ascorbic-acid (100 mg/kg) comparator.

Reported observations

Compared with the UV-exposed group, the paper reports lower skin weight, epidermal thickness, wrinkle and fine-wrinkle measures and changes in type-I-collagen and hyaluronic-acid-related measures. Group body weights did not differ.
