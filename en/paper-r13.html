OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R13 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Ginseng flower, berry and leaf extraction comparison

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R13 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Ryu Hee-Jeong, Jung Chul-Jong, Beik Gyung-Yun. “Antioxidant activities of flower, berry and leaf of Panax ginseng C. A. Meyer.” Korean Journal of Food Science and Technology 52(4), 342–349 (2020). DOI: 10.9721/KJFST.2020.52.4.342.

Publisher DOI · KoreaScience article

Material and model

Panax ginseng flowers, berries and leaves were extracted in hot water and in 60% ethanol. KoreaScience lists all three authors with Okchundang’s R&D laboratory.

The extracts were compared for total polyphenols, flavonoids and crude saponins, and for DPPH, ABTS and H₂O₂ scavenging, reducing power and lipid-peroxidation measures.

Reported observations

The abstract reports that ginseng-leaf hot-water and 60% ethanol extracts ranked higher in polyphenol/flavonoid content and several in-vitro antioxidant assays than the compared flower and berry extracts.
