OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R03 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Kyeongok-go alone: experimental cough counts

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R03 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jin-Ryul Hu, Chul-Jong Jung, Seong-Min Ku, Dae-Hwa Jung, Khawaja Muhammad Imran Bashir, Sae-Kwang Ku, Jae-Suk Choi. “Anti-inflammatory, expectorant, and antitussive properties of Kyeongok-go in ICR mice.” Pharmaceutical Biology 59(1) (2021), 319–332. DOI: 10.1080/13880209.2021.1892155.

Publisher DOI · Full text (PMC)

Material and model

Kyeongok-go (KOG) contained ginseng, Poria, fresh Rehmannia and honey. The paper describes mixing the ingredients and repeating a 72-hour heating/cooling process in a 60°C water bath twice; sa-sam was not part of this KOG preparation.

ICR mice (eight per group) received 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg orally daily for 11 days. Coughs were counted for six minutes after ammonia challenge; tracheal phenol-red optical density and xylene-induced ear measures were assessed separately.

Reported observations

At 400 mg/kg the paper reports 59.53% fewer coughs than the ammonia control, 31.29% and 30.72% lower relative and absolute ear weights than the xylene control, and 39.06% higher tracheal phenol-red optical density than the untreated control. These percentages refer to different endpoints and comparison groups.
