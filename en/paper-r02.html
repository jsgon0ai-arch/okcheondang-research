OKCHEONDANG / PAPER-R02 — RESEARCH RECORDS

Sasam-Kyeongokgo in mouse cough and inflammation models

Botanical research.
Methods & records.

Publication R02 summarizes its study material, experimental design and reported observations.

Citation and source

Jin-Ryul Hu, Chul-Jong Jung, Seong-Min Ku, Dae-Hwa Jung, Sae-Kwang Ku, Md. Mohibbullah, Hae-Jeung Lee, Jae-Suk Choi. “Deciphering the Antitussive, Expectorant, and Anti-Inflammatory Potentials of ShashamKyeongok-Go and Their Phytochemical Attributes: In Vivo Appraisal in ICR Mice.” Applied Sciences 11(3) (2021), 1349. DOI: 10.3390/app11031349.

Publisher DOI · Publisher record

Material and model

ShashamKyeongok-Go (SKOG) combined sa-sam with Kyeongok-go. The paper describes its sa-sam component as dried Adenophora triphylla var. japonica root; this preparation differs from KOG alone in R03.

ICR mice received SKOG at 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg orally once daily for 11 days. Investigators separately measured ammonia-induced cough, tracheal phenol-red optical density and xylene-induced ear inflammation.

Reported observations

The publisher text reports fewer induced cough responses and reductions in ear weight, tissue thickness and inflammatory-cell infiltration compared with challenged controls.
